About SKYgirlsBW

A movement empowering girls to be confident, independent and bold.

Who We Are

SKYgirlsBW is a youth empowerment movement that helps teenage girls make informed choices, build confidence and resist peer pressure.

We use storytelling, media and community engagement to create real impact across Botswana.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To empower girls to make confident, informed decisions and build self-worth.

  • Build confidence
  • Encourage independence
  • Promote healthy choices
  • Create safe spaces

Vision

A generation of confident young women leading change in their communities.

  • Empowered girls
  • Strong leadership
  • Positive communities
  • Continental impact

Our Journey

2014 - Launch

SKYgirlsBW officially launched in Botswana as a youth movement.

2016 - Expansion

Introduced radio dramas and school outreach programs.

2020 - Digital Growth

Expanded online presence and social media engagement.
